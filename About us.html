ABOUT US

Quartz is more than just the name of our section. It represents our growth, potential, experiences, and memories that we create together throughout the school year.

Just like quartz, each member of our section has their own individual qualities. We may have different personalities, talents, interests, and strengths. But together, we form a family.

Our Handsign

Our handsign represents who we are.

The curled finger in the middle, boxed in green, symbolizes you as a piece of charcoal. Although it may seem ordinary and of little value at first, it possesses the potential to become something extraordinary.

The two fingers surrounding the charcoal represent your safe space — a place where you gain experience, develop your strengths, and gradually transform into the unique form of quartz you are destined to become.

Our handsign reminds us that growth takes time. With the right environment, experiences, support, and determination, we can develop our potential and become the best version of ourselves.

Keep growing, keep achieving, and keep shining ✨
